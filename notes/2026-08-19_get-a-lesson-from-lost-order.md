# If You Can’t Get the Order, Get a Lesson

## 内容

来源：NickWhite 销售培训截图。

If you can’t get the order, get a lesson.

When you find out that the buyer is giving the order to one of your competitors, instead of saying things like “such a pity” and “if you would’ve let me know, we could’ve given you a better price” which gains you nothing, at least you can get a lesson learned.

Say:

> I totally understand and I’m sure you have your reasons. Would you mind sharing with me where we miss the Mark and why you choose that supplier?

Sometimes you will get valuable feedback that helps you improve your offer for next time so you have a higher chance of winning in the future.

## Bubble 的洞察

- 这条的价值在于把“输单”从情绪事件改造成信息资产：真正成熟的销售不是在失去订单后辩解或补价，而是用低姿态换取客户决策逻辑，让每一次 loss 都成为下一次 offer 的校准数据。
